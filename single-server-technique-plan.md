# Wine pour — 6 Figure Server technique (awaiting approval)

## What his video actually shows
Source studied: 6 Figure Server, "How to Serve Wine Like a Pro — Restaurant Server Training" (youtube.com/watch?v=1mQNrFWJaQA), frames reviewed at 1:44–2:40.

1. **Serviette on the left arm.** Folded cloth draped over the bent left forearm before the pour ("that leaves both hands free").
2. **Grip at the base.** Right hand holds the lower body of the bottle near the punt, never the neck or shoulder. The bottle sits almost level.
3. **Label to the guest.** The label faces the person being poured for the whole time.
4. **Pour into the center of the bowl.** The bottle mouth stays just above the rim and never touches the glass. About 3–5 oz per guest.
5. **Twist and lift.** When the pour stops, the wrist turns a little and the neck comes up so the last drop doesn't fall.
6. **Wipe the lip.** The left hand brings the serviette up to the mouth of the upright bottle and wipes it.
7. **Drape it back.** The serviette goes back over the left forearm, and the hands go back to rest.
